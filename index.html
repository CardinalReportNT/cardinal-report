🚨 Breaking — The Cardinal Report

Preston Officially Day-to-Day as Possible Return Nears

Preston has been activated from the 15-Day IL and is officially DAY-TO-DAY as he continues working his way back from a strained tricep. He has not been cleared to play yet.

At approximately 10:47 AM today, Preston spoke with Cardinals coaching staff and informed them that he feels ready to return to the lineup. The brief call ended around 10:48 AM, with coaches telling him they’ll make a decision following Wednesday’s practice.

Preston has already returned to light baseball activity and has spent recent games coaching third base while sidelined.

Before the injury, Preston was 1-for-1 with 1 RBI, 1 BB and 0 K, reaching base in both plate appearances.

The Cardinals aren’t getting their pitcher back just yet — but they could be getting one of their biggest bats and a key piece of their infield back. 👀

If Wednesday goes well, Thursday is the first possible return date. If more time is needed, Saturday, Sept. 12 becomes the next target. Nothing is official yet.
